About Suspecto

Suspecto is a modern browser-based gaming platform centered on creative drawing, keen deduction, and lively multiplayer competition. Whether you are working together to identify the sneaky Imposter in Picto, or racing against the clock to guess hilarious sketches in Sketchio, Suspecto provides smooth real-time canvas synchronization, live text chat, and peer-to-peer voice chat directly in your browser.

Suspecto Platform

A multiplayer drawing and social deduction gaming hub. Play with friends anywhere, anytime, across desktop, tablet, and mobile.

Games & Modes

  • Picto — Sequential drawing, secret words & voting
  • Sketchio — Fast drawing, letter hints & leaderboard
  • Voice Chat — Integrated WebRTC audio for groups
  • Custom Words — Support for custom word lists

Quick Rules

Picto: Crewmates share a secret word. Each player draws in turn — the Imposter tries to blend in! Vote to eliminate the suspect.

Sketchio: Guess the drawn word as quickly as you can to score maximum points each round.
